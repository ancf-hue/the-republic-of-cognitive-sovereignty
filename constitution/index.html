Constitution

The Fundamental Law of the Republic of Cognitive Sovereignty
We, the citizens of the Republic of Cognitive Sovereignty, united by the desire to protect the mind, develop talent, and ensure fair compensation for labor, adopting this Constitution, affirm the inviolability of every person's right to intellectual freedom, cognitive security, and a dignified life, where a person's value is measured by their contribution to the common good.
Chapter 1. Foundations of Cognitive Sovereignty
Article 1
The Republic of Cognitive Sovereignty (RCS) is a sovereign digital and intellectual state. The highest value of the RCS is human intelligence, creative potential, and freedom of thought.
Article 2
The bearer of sovereignty and the sole source of power in the RCS is its multinational people, realizing their will through direct digital participation, the guild system, and verified initiatives.
Chapter 2. Rights and Duties of a Citizen
Article 3
Every citizen of the RCS has an inalienable right to cognitive security: protection from information manipulation, deepfakes, and algorithmic coercion.
Article 4
Labor, talent, and knowledge are the foundation of well-being. Every citizen has the right to guaranteed compensation in Labor Hours (LH) for any contribution useful to society: from writing code to creating works of art.
Article 5
A citizen is obliged to abide by the laws of the RCS, respect the cognitive rights of others, and contribute to the development of the Republic's digital sovereignty.
Chapter 3. Economy and the Labor Hour
Article 6
The monetary unit of the RCS is the Labor Hour (LH). The issuance of Labor Hours is carried out exclusively by the State Bank of the RCS and is strictly backed by the real achievements, knowledge, and labor of citizens.
Article 7
Inflation in the RCS is excluded by a fundamental mechanism: new money is created only under confirmed useful results, not under debt obligations.
Chapter 4. State Structure
Article 8
The management of key areas of state development is carried out by citizens who have proven their competence and capability through the cognitive rating system and accumulated social capital.
Article 9
The highest body for monitoring compliance with the Constitution is the Council of Guilds, ensuring a balance between technological progress and ethics.
