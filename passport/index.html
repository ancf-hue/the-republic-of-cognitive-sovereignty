🔍 How to create a passport?

Step 1: Go to the "Verification" tab.

Step 2: Enter Full Name and Date of Birth.

Step 3: Upload a video with head rotation (left-right).

Step 4: Click "Analyze" — AI will verify the identity.

Step 5: Upon success, the passport unlocks, and the state grants 1 Labor Hour.
